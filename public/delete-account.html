LinguaVox

Delete your account

You can delete your LinguaVox account and all your data at any time. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open LinguaVox and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon, then Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the app may ask you to log out and log back in first, then try again.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email mikplatforms@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject Delete my LinguaVox account. We will delete your account and data and confirm by email within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, bio, avatar, settings)
  • Your chat conversations and messages
  • Your learning memory, progress, XP, streaks, and study plans
  • Your login

Data held briefly by service providers while an AI reply is generated, or in routine technical logs, follows those providers' own retention practices. See our Privacy Policy.
