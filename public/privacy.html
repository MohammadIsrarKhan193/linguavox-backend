LinguaVox

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

LinguaVox is an AI-powered language learning app made by MÎK Platforms ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up or sign in we receive your email address and your name. If you sign in with Google we also receive your Google profile photo link.

Profile information you choose to add. Your username, bio, country, native language, learning language, English level, learning goal, and the avatar you pick.

Learning activity. Your chat conversations with the AI tutors (your messages and the tutors' replies, with the time each was sent), your lesson and course progress, XP, streaks, practice time, fluency score, and the strengths and mistakes the app notes so your tutor can help you better next time ("learning memory").

Voice input. If you use the microphone, your speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition service. LinguaVox does not record or store audio files; only the resulting text is used, like a typed message.

Technical information. Our servers and service providers may process basic technical data, such as your device type and error logs, to keep the app running and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To run AI conversations and lessons, and to give feedback.
  • To remember your progress and personalise what each tutor teaches you.
  • To show your XP, streaks, and skill progress.
  • To fix bugs, prevent abuse, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and LinguaVox does not show third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your account and learning data.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Our AI backend, hosted on Render, which sends the text of your messages (and the lesson context needed for a good reply) to an AI model provider, currently Groq, to generate the tutor's answer. Those providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Your device's speech recognition and text-to-speech services (for example Google or Apple), when you use voice. Their handling is governed by your device settings and their policies.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the app.

4. Keeping your data and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists so your progress and tutor memory are there whenever you return.

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, chats, learning memory, progress, and study plans from our database, and then deletes your login. For your security, if you signed in a while ago the app may ask you to sign out and back in first. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you can't open the app, you can also request deletion on our page Delete your account or by emailing mikplatforms@gmail.com from the email address of your account.

Data held by an AI provider while a reply is being generated, or in routine technical logs, is governed by that provider's retention practices.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile information in Profile → Edit.
  • You can change your language, level, and tutor in Settings.
  • You can delete your account and data as described above.
  • You can ask us questions or request a copy of your data by contacting us below. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to some processing. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Security

Data moves between the app and our services over encrypted connections, and access to your data in our database is limited to your own account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device protected.

7. Children

LinguaVox is not directed to children under 13 [confirm your minimum age], and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International use

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. By using the app you understand your information may be transferred and processed there.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for important changes we will tell you in the app.
