LinguaVox

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

LinguaVox is an AI English tutor app made by MÎK Platforms ("we", "us"). This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, who handles it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, from Google Sign-In or when you sign up with email.
  • Profile details you choose to add: username, bio, country, native language, learning goal, English level and avatar.
  • Your chats with the AI tutors: the messages you send, the tutors' replies, and the time of each.
  • Your learning progress: lessons completed, XP, streaks, practice time, fluency score, and the strengths and mistakes the app notes so your tutor can help you better.
  • Voice: if you use the microphone, your device's speech recognition turns your speech into text. LinguaVox does not record or store audio files.
  • Technical information: our service providers (see section 4) may process basic technical data, such as IP address and device type, to run and secure the app.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and keep it secure.
  • To run your AI conversations and lessons and give you feedback.
  • To save your progress and personalise what each tutor teaches you.
  • To fix problems and keep the service working.

3. How the AI tutors work

When you send a message, the text of your message (plus a short part of the recent conversation and your lesson context) is sent from our server to Groq, our AI provider, to write the tutor's reply. Your conversation is then saved in your account in Firebase so you can see your chat history. Groq handles that text under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Services we use

  • Firebase Authentication for sign-in, and Cloud Firestore to store your profile, chats and progress (Google).
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it.
  • Render, which hosts our server.
  • Groq, which generates AI replies.
  • Your device's speech recognition and text-to-speech (for example Google or Apple) when you use voice.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your chat history with advertisers. LinguaVox shows no third-party ads.
  • We do not record or store your voice as audio.

6. Your rights and deleting your account

You can edit your profile in Profile → Edit.

You can delete your account at any time: go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, chats, learning progress and study plans from our database, and then deletes your login. It cannot be undone. For your security, if you signed in a while ago the app may ask you to log out and back in first.

If you can't open the app, you can request deletion on our Delete your account page or by emailing mikplatforms@gmail.com from the email address of your account. Data held briefly by our providers while a reply is generated, or in routine technical logs, follows their own retention practices.

You may also ask us for a copy of your data or ask a question about it at the email below.

7. Security

Data travels between the app and our services over encrypted connections, and each user can only access their own data in our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

8. Children

LinguaVox is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
