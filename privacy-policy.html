LinguaVox ("we", "our", "the app") is developed by MÎK Platforms. This policy explains what information we collect when you use LinguaVox, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name and email address, either entered directly or provided by Google Sign-In (which may also share your Google profile photo if you choose to use it).

Learning data. To provide tutoring, we store your conversations with tutors, lesson progress, XP, streaks, and a summary of patterns we notice (such as recurring mistakes) so tutors can personalize future lessons. You can review and manage what LinguaVox remembers about you in the app's Memory settings.

Voice input. If you use voice conversation, your speech is converted to text on your device or via your device's speech recognition service so you can talk to a tutor. We do not store audio recordings — only the resulting text becomes part of your conversation, the same as a typed message.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize your English tutoring experience
  • To track your learning progress, streaks, and achievements
  • To generate AI tutor responses based on your conversation history
  • To maintain your account and keep your progress synced across sessions

Third-Party Services

LinguaVox relies on the following services to operate:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, account storage, and your learning data (Firestore database).
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method, governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Groq — processes your conversation messages to generate your AI tutor's replies. Message content is sent to Groq's API for this purpose and is not used by Groq to train models on our behalf.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account is active, so your progress is preserved between sessions. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at [your support email]. We will delete your data within a reasonable time after a verified request, except where we are required to retain limited information for legal or security purposes.

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for optional features (such as voice input) at any time via your device permissions

Children's Privacy

LinguaVox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Security

Your data is transmitted using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and stored using Firebase's security infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as LinguaVox evolves. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at [your support email].
